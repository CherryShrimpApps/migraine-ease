Migraine Ease – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

In short: Migraine Ease keeps your health logs on your phone. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

This policy explains how the Migraine Ease app for Android ("the app") handles information. The app is provided by CherryShrimp Apps ("we", "us"). Questions can be sent to CherryShrimpApps@gmail.com.

1. Information you enter

When you log an attack, you may enter details such as the date and time, severity, pain location, symptoms, triggers and medication. This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. We cannot see it and we never receive it.

2. Phone sensors

If your phone has an air pressure sensor (barometer) or a light sensor, the app reads them only while the app is open. The readings are saved with your logs, and a short history of air pressure readings (about the last 7 days) is kept for the pressure trend. All of this stays on your phone. The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or any other personal data.

3. No data sent to us or to third parties

The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools. It does not contain code that sends your logs to any server, and we do not sell or share personal information.

4. Reports, exports and backups you create

The app can create a Doctor Visit PDF, a CSV spreadsheet and a backup file. These files are saved only where you choose, using Android's own file screen. They contain your health information, so please keep them somewhere safe. If you share a report with your doctor or anyone else, that is your own action and is outside the app.

5. Android phone backup

Android may include this app's data in the automatic backup of your own Google account if you have phone backup turned on. That backup is managed by Google and your phone settings, and it is covered by Google's own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or have access to it. You can turn phone backup off in your Android settings.

6. Purchases

The app may offer optional one-time purchases (a "Pro" unlock and optional supporter tips). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether a purchase exists, so that features you bought stay unlocked.

7. Children

The app is intended for adults. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8. Your control and deletion

You can edit or delete any log inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes its data from your phone. Files you exported, and any copies kept by Android or Google backup, stay under your control and can be deleted by you.

9. Not medical advice

Migraine Ease is a personal record-keeping tool. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition and does not replace advice from a doctor or pharmacist.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
